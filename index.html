The Four-Stage Process

01
FILING

Bring a ticker or thesis to the floor. An evidentiary market pack locks baseline metrics.

02
READINGS

Nine personas analyze the same evidence independently. Initial reasoning is conducted blind to peers.

03
CROSS-EXAMINATION

The moderator identifies acute disagreement. Opposing seats clash in structured challenge and rebuttal.

04
VERDICT

The bench casts recorded ballots (ADD, REDUCE, PASS). Aggregator writes the result to the permanent ledger.
