    Protocol & Reasoning Principles

    How nine competing minds produce one institutional verdict.

    1. Epistemic Independence

    The greatest failure mode of investment committees is premature consensus. In Bourse Chamber, each persona evaluates the locked Evidentiary Market Pack in complete epistemic isolation during Round 1.

    Epistemic Rule No seat observes the reasoning or confidence of another seat until its own preliminary read is formally filed to the transcript.

    2. Structural Cross-Examination & Friction

    Bourse Chamber is designed to introduce healthy cognitive friction. Users are forced to confront the strongest arguments against their own thesis before taking action elsewhere. In Round 2, ideological extremes duel directly across the floor.

    Duel Rule Cross-examination is not an argument over adjectives. It is a confrontation over which assumption breaks first under adverse market regimes.

    3. Taleb Exclusive Position Sizing Mandate

    Conviction without survivable sizing is portfolio fragility. Nassim Nicholas Taleb (Seat 06) is strictly configured as the sole authority dictating the Position Size Band, applying barbell containment to prevent absorbing ruin barriers.

    Sizing Principle "At half a percent, wrong is a bad week. At four percent, wrong is the year. Vote is on survivable sizing, not on the forecast."

    4. Legal Disclaimer & Simulation Boundaries

    Tier 1 — Non-Affiliation: The personas represented (Benjamin Graham, Charlie Munger, Peter Lynch, Cathie Wood, Aswath Damodaran, Nassim Nicholas Taleb, Mohnish Pabrai, Bill Ackman, Michael Burry) are conceptual reasoning models derived from public writings and investment philosophies. They do not represent, endorse, or affiliate with this application.

    Tier 2 — Non-Financial Advice: Bourse Chamber is an educational reasoning simulator. Outputs (ADD, REDUCE, PASS, sizing bands) are philosophical simulations and do not constitute personalized financial, investment, legal, or tax advice.

    Tier 3 — Simulated Evidentiary Snapshots: Market data snapshots and on-chain metrics displayed during sessions are labeled demonstration data and should never be used as real-time pricing feeds for capital execution.
