Legal Disclaimer & Persona Boundaries

Formal boundaries, non-affiliation disclosures, and educational definitions.
IMPORTANT NOTICE: Bourse Chamber is an educational multi-agent simulation and analytical thought experiment. It does not provide personalized investment advice, price targets, order execution, or financial planning.

1. Nature of the AI Personas

The nine personas participating in the chamber are artificial intelligence models constructed from publicly documented investing philosophies, published academic books, investor letters, and historical treatises.

They are not the living or historical individuals, do not speak for them, have no affiliation with them or their respective firms, and do not represent their personal or professional views. No endorsement, sponsorship, or partnership is implied or exists.

2. No Financial Advice or Price Predictions

Nothing contained on Bourse Chamber constitutes investment, legal, tax, or accounting advice. Digital assets and cryptocurrencies are volatile, speculative, and carry significant risk of permanent capital impairment.

The Position Size Bands, dissenting opinions, and recorded ballots (ADD / REDUCE / PASS) are heuristic reasoning outputs intended to illustrate multi-disciplinary debate, not personalized recommendations to purchase, hold, or liquidate any security or token.

3. Product Limitations

Bourse Chamber does not hold custody of user funds, does not connect to external crypto wallets, does not integrate with trading brokerages, and does not execute financial transactions.

AI personas built from published investing philosophies — not the individuals, not financial advice.
